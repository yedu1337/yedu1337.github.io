# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **yedukrishna012@gmail.com** with:

- a description of the issue
- steps to reproduce it
- the impact you observed
- any suggested remediation

Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. If you need an encrypted channel, say so in your first message and we can arrange one. Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate data that is not yours.

## Scope

In scope:

- this portfolio site and its build/deployment configuration
- [Ghost-Proxy](https://github.com/yedu1337/Ghost-Proxy)
- [Guardian-Eye](https://github.com/yedu1337/Guardian-Eye)

Out of scope:

- third-party services and dependencies
- social engineering, physical attacks, and denial-of-service testing
- any system I do not own or have explicit permission to test
- intentionally vulnerable lab targets

## Safe harbor

Good-faith research that respects privacy and does not disrupt service is welcome. I will not pursue legal action for accidental issues caused by research conducted within this scope. This is not a bug bounty program.

## What to expect

I aim to acknowledge reports within a few days, investigate, and keep you updated. If the issue is valid, I will fix it and credit you unless you prefer to remain anonymous.

## Machine-readable policy

The canonical policy is published at:

- `https://yedu1337.github.io/.well-known/security.txt`
- `https://yedu1337.github.io/security/`
